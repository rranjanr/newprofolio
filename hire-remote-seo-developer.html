Questions US companies ask

Can you really work US business hours?

Yes. Nepal is UTC+5:45, roughly 9 hours 45 minutes ahead of US Eastern in summer and 10 hours 45 minutes otherwise. I already work with US-based companies on that schedule and keep overlapping hours for meetings and deploys. Async work happens while you sleep.

Why a generalist instead of specialists?

Most delay in marketing work is handoff, not execution. An SEO finding that needs a code change normally waits in a dev queue. I do both, so it ships the same day. For smaller teams it is also considerably cheaper than four specialists or an agency retainer.
